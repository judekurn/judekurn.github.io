# About Jude H. Kurniawan, PhD

Dr. Jude H. Kurniawan is a sustainability transitions scholar and educator specializing in complexity science, system dynamics, and transition management. His work operates at the intersection of socio-technical transitions, social-ecological systems, urban sustainability, and environmental and climate policy & governance to transform sustainability from an abstract concept into a navigable system. As an educator, he is dedicated to authentic, experential (work-integrated) learning, using systems thinking to bridge technical and social disciplines.
