Part 3: Create-Destroy-Recreate (CDR) Lab

RECUP is Germany's largest reusable takeaway cup and food-container network. In this condensed teaching-demo version, students move from model creation to validation, critique, and refinement.

Step 1: Create the Baseline Model

Open the instructor prompt. Copy the prompt and paste it into Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI prototyping platform. Generate the baseline RECUP dashboard before proceeding.

Output: Generate a working RECUP dashboard before continuing to Step 2.

Step 2: Validate the Model

  • Locate the incorrect Trucost reference year.
  • Identify the incorrectly connected edge (N5 should connect to N1, not N2).
  • Look for any additional inconsistencies or modelling errors.

Step 3: AI-Assisted Critique

Ask Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT:

Review this model. What important variables, relationships, or feedback loops may be missing?

Step 4: Human Critique and Refinement

  • Which AI suggestions should be accepted?
  • Which suggestions should be rejected?
  • What important variables or feedback loops are still missing?
  • How would your additions change system behaviour?
