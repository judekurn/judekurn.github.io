01 Variables & Causal Links

Variables represent measurable quantities or states (such as Partner Density or Hoarded Cup Stock) that can increase or decrease. Directed arrows denote causal influence from cause to effect.

+ Positive (+) vs. − Negative (−) Polarity

A + link means variables move in the same direction (all else equal). A − link means an increase in the source drives a decrease in the target (an inverse change).

The Even/Odd Negative-Link Rule

Tracing a perturbation around a closed loop flips its sign every time it crosses a negative (−) link and preserves its sign across a positive (+) link:

  • Even − Links (0, 2, 4, ...): Sign returns unchanged → Reinforcing (R) amplification.
  • Odd − Links (1, 3, 5, ...): Sign reverses once around the cycle → Balancing (B) counteraction.

Self-Amplifying Dynamics

Reinforcing Loops

Reinforcing loops (R) amplify change in whichever direction it begins—producing a virtuous cycle of rapid expansion or a vicious cycle of contraction.

R1: N1 → N2 → N3 → N4 → N1

R1: Network Expansion Loop

Type: Reinforcing (R)

Higher partner density improves return convenience, driving adoption volume and vendor financial savings, which incentivizes more partners to join.

Counteracting & Stabilizing Dynamics

Balancing Loops

Balancing loops (B) counteract change, regulate system limits, and push variables toward a constrained equilibrium.

B1: N3 → N6 → N5 → N1 → N2 → N3

B1: Cup Hoarding Constraint

Type: Balancing (B)

Increased adoption leads to cup hoarding, which reduces circulating stock, lowers return convenience, and dampens adoption.

Vibe Coding Methodology

AI Rapid Prototyping

Vibe Coding pairs natural-language specifications with rapid iteration to turn systems models into interactive web dashboards—while keeping human systems judgment in charge of correctness.

STEP 01

Prompt Architecture

Specify exact variables, polarities, loop sequences, visual tokens, and interactive controls up front so the model has unambiguous structural constraints.

STEP 02

Iterative Generation

Generate a self-contained HTML/CSS/JS prototype quickly, inspect the live preview, and issue targeted follow-up prompts for layout or simulation refinements.

STEP 03

Human-in-the-Loop Audit

Audit loop closure, polarity counts, SVG arrow clearances, mobile layout, and edge cases—AI accelerates drafting, while domain experts verify truth.
