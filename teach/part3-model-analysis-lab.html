Part 3: Create-Destroy-Recreate (CDR) Lab

Background

RECUP is Germany's largest reusable takeaway cup and food-container network. Customers pay a small deposit, borrow a reusable cup from participating cafés and retailers, and return it to any partner location within the network. The model seeks to reduce single-use packaging waste through reuse, reverse logistics, and network effects.

Is Germany's RECUP reusable cup system genuinely resilient under alternative climate futures? Or does its performance depend on assumptions that may not hold under real-world conditions?
