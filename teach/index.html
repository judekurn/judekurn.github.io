Systems Thinking, Sustainability Modelling & AI-Assisted Learning

This lesson demonstrates how selected elements of experiential inquiry can be recreated within the classroom through systems mapping, scenario stress-testing, and AI-assisted rapid prototyping. Students move through a Create-Destroy-Recreate (CDR) learning cycle in which they construct a model, challenge its assumptions, and rebuild a more comprehensive representation of a sustainability system.

Create → Destroy → Recreate (CDR)

This lesson demonstrates a Create-Destroy-Recreate (CDR) learning workflow inspired by iterative systems simulation and design practice. Rather than memorising sustainability concepts, students repeatedly construct, challenge, and improve their understanding of complex sustainability challenges.

Create

Build an initial model of a sustainability system through systems mapping, causal loop diagrams, futures thinking, and AI-assisted rapid prototyping.

Destroy

Challenge the initial model by introducing new evidence, alternative perspectives, future scenarios, bottlenecks, unintended consequences, and system shocks.

Recreate

Reconstruct the model to produce a more robust understanding of the system and identify sustainability interventions, leverage points, and policies.

Important: Students are not assessed on the sophistication of the dashboard itself. The dashboard is a temporary learning artefact designed to make systems reasoning visible. Assessment focuses on:
  • Systems Thinking
  • Evidence Gathering
  • Scenario Analysis
  • Assumption Testing
  • Critical Reflection
Repeated participation in the CDR cycle helps students build a portfolio of analytical artefacts that can be showcased to prospective employers, graduate programmes, and professional sustainability organizations.

Learning Outcomes

  • Construct a systems-based representation of a sustainability challenge.
  • Identify assumptions that influence system behaviour and evaluate how changing those assumptions affects sustainability outcomes.
  • Stress-test system performance under alternative climate and socioeconomic futures.
  • Revise and improve system models using evidence and systems reasoning.
  • Evaluate the resilience, trade-offs, and limitations of sustainability interventions.
