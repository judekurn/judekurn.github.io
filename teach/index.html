Systems Thinking, Sustainability Modelling & AI-Assisted Learning

Demonstration sequence developed for sustainability education. The activities progressively move students from systems thinking, to sustainability modelling, to model revision, to student-generated analytical tools, and finally to critical reflection on the theoretical limits of circularity.

Learning Journey

Part 1 → Understand Feedback Systems
Part 2 → Model a Circular Economy Intervention
Part 3 → Revise Assumptions Using Evidence
Part 4 → Examine Student-Created Outputs
Part 5 → Reflect on the Limits of Circularity

Part 1: Systems Thinking (Causal Loop Diagram)
