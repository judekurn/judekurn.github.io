# Cross-Impact Balance (CIB) for Climate Scenarios

Cross-Impact Balance (CIB) provides a rigorous, transparent method for qualitative-to-quantitative downscaling without relying on opaque, multi-million dollar computational climate models. 

## Systemic Matrix Dynamics
Instead of running chaotic atmospheric simulations, CIB evaluates the interconnected matrix of socio-technical, regulatory, and physical variables. It systematically isolates and filters out contradictory futures, leaving decision-makers with internally consistent, mathematically validated scenarios.

### Application in Enterprise Strategy
* **Systemic Interdependencies:** Maps out how regulatory changes directly impact supply chain path dependencies.
* **Algorithmic Consistency:** Replaces subjective "guessing" with a structured cross-impact matrix.
